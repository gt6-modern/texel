
# Governance

Texel is maintained by the GT6 Modern organization. Decisions are made in public issues and pull requests whenever confidentiality does not require otherwise. Language changes require a normative specification change, conformance cases, and agreement between both independent compiler implementations. Maintainers may merge after required checks pass; releases require an organization maintainer with access to the protected release environments. The project is pre-1.0 and follows semantic versioning.
